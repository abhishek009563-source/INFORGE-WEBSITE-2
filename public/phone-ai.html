help_outline How to Connect Your Android Phone via USB:
  1. Connect your Android Phone to your PC using a USB cable.
  2. Go to Settings $\rightarrow$ About Phone on your phone and tap Build Number 7 times to enable Developer Options.
  3. Go to Developer Options and enable USB Debugging.
  4. When your phone asks "Allow USB debugging?", check Always allow and click Allow.
