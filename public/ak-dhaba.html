// OUR STORY

Hearty Hospitality & Time-Honored Punjabi Recipes

At AK Dhaba, we bring the warmth and rich culinary heritage of Punjab straight to your table. Founded on a passion for authentic taste, we prepare every dish using secret family spice blends, traditional clay tandoors, and fresh ingredients.

Whether it's our slow-cooked Dal Makhani simmered overnight or our signature Butter Chicken in rich makhani gravy, we believe in generous portions, hearty flavours, and memorable dining experiences with family and friends.
